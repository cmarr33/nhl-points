How the model works
  • Bayesian updating (conjugate priors) — points, rebounds and assists each have a per-minute Poisson rate with a Gamma prior, with minutes played as the exposure. The prior is last season's per-minute rate, counted as if it were 1 game of minutes. This season's games update it exactly (Gamma + Poisson stays Gamma), with recent games weighted more (half-life 6 games). The "Bayes rate" is the posterior PRA. The ± is how uncertain that rate still is.
  • Minutes model — projection = projected minutes × per-minute rates. Minutes are a recency-weighted average (half-life 4 games) of the player's "full-roster" minutes: each past game minus any extra minutes he got that night because teammates sat. When rotation teammates (15+ minutes a game) who just played are listed Out or Doubtful tonight, 40% of their minutes are shared among the active players according to each player's headroom (how far below his recent high he plays); the rest goes to the deep bench. The minutes spread feeds the game-level factor of the multivariate Poisson. Tuned on the 2025-26 backtest.
  • Form — the posterior rate minus the prior: how far this season's games have moved the player from last season. The sparkline shows the posterior after each recent game, and the dashed line is the prior.
  • Opponent pace & defense — team ratings built from every player's box scores league-wide: possessions per game (pace) and points, rebounds and assists allowed per possession (defense), weighted toward recent games (half-life 15) and shrunk toward league average by 10 pseudo-games. Tonight's opponent is compared with the opponents the player usually faces: pace = (his team's pace + opponent's) ÷ (his team's + his usual opponents'), and defense = the opponent's per-possession allowed ÷ his usual opponents'. Each stat's rate is multiplied by both. The column shows the PRA effect. On the 2025-26 backtest this improved every score.
  • Head-to-head — the player's past PRA against tonight's opponent, shown for context only. Splits built from a handful of games added noise in the backtest, so team-level defense is used instead.
  • Home / away — home PRA minus away PRA (H−A), also shown for context and not applied, for the same reason.
  • Multivariate Poisson — PRA isn't three independent counts. Each pair of stats shares a common-shock Poisson term (a bivariate Poisson per pair, e.g. a made basket off a rebound), fitted from the player's covariances. On top of that, a game-level Gamma factor for minutes, pace and blowouts moves all three together. That factor's size (φ) comes from how much more the player's stats swing than a plain Poisson would, plus the uncertainty left in the Bayesian posterior.
  • Probability — the server computes the full PRA distribution from that model, so P(PRA > line) is read directly from it at any line. That probability is then calibrated with Platt scaling (calibrate.py), fitted on last season's walk-forward backtest at many lines, so a stated 60% hits about 60% of the time.
  • Backtest — settings were chosen by replaying all of 2025-26 (about 20,000 player-games), predicting each game from earlier games only (backtest.py). Prior strength and recency were tuned there, and the splits were switched off there.
  • Combos — each leg is an OVER or UNDER call on the player's current line, whichever side the model favors. Low Risk - Low Reward takes the most confident calls. High Risk - High Reward takes volatile players (above-median spread) with a 55–70% lean, ranked by the size of the projected edge. Suggested combos use at most one player per team and two per game. You can flip a pick, replace (⇄) or remove (✕) a player, add players (up to 10) or start a new combo, and the odds update. Edits are saved in this browser for that date. Hit chance multiplies the legs' probabilities, treating the games as independent. Fair payout is 1 ÷ hit chance. Kalshi-implied payout appears when every leg has a Kalshi price at its line.
  • Daily refresh — while the server is running it rebuilds today's slate at midnight and again at 9:00 AM, after late games are final. An open page moves to the new day by itself and reloads every 10 minutes.

Data: ESPN public endpoints. Betting lines: Kalshi public markets. Projections are statistical estimates, not guarantees.
